Privacy Policy

App: Earning Rusher
Developer: Yash Chouhan (India)
Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Earning Rusher ("the app") collects, how we use it, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, surname, email address and a password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication, we cannot see your password).
  • App account ID: a unique user ID (UID) and referral code, created when you register.
  • Activity: daily check-in, spin and quiz results, coins earned, referral activity, and the days you opened the app (used to count active users).
  • Payment details (only if you withdraw): UPI ID or bank account name, account number and IFSC code, entered by you.
  • Support messages: the text you send to customer support.
  • Device identifier: a random ID stored in your browser/app storage, used to limit one account per device.
  • Profile photo: only if you upload one.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account, track coins and process withdrawal requests.
  • To show offers, and to credit coins when an offer is completed.
  • To show advertisements and measure app usage.
  • To prevent fraud and multiple accounts.
  • To answer your support requests.

3. Third-party services

We use these services, which may process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, analytics).
  • Google AdMob (advertisements). AdMob may use advertising identifiers on your device. You can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Perkox and other offer partners, who receive your user ID when you open their offers, and send us the completion details.
  • Cloudflare (our secure server that receives offer completion messages).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the services above, or when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and data, email us at the address below from the email you registered with. We will delete your data within a reasonable time, except records we must keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use Firebase security rules and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, so please keep your password private.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
