About Earning Rusher

Earning Rusher is a rewards app where you can earn coins by doing simple things: checking in daily, watching short video ads, spinning the daily wheel, completing offers, and inviting friends. Coins can be converted into real money and withdrawn to your UPI or bank account.

How it works

  • Daily check-in: claim a few coins every day. The reward grows for 7 days, then the cycle restarts.
  • Watch & Earn: watch short video ads and earn coins for each one.
  • Daily Spin: spin the wheel once a day and win coins.
  • Tasks: complete offers from our partner networks. Coins are added after the offer is verified.
  • Refer & Earn: invite friends with your referral link.

Withdrawals

10 coins = ₹1. Your first withdrawal is ₹10. After that, withdrawals open on the 1st and 2nd of every month, with a minimum of ₹25. Requests are reviewed by our team and paid to your UPI or bank account.

Fair use

One account per device. Multiple accounts, fake activity or misuse of offers can lead to a ban and loss of coins.
